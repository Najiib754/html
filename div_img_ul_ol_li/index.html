Nelson Mandela

  • Full Name: Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela
  • Born: July 18, 1918
  • Died: December 5, 2013
  • Nationality: South African
  • Profession: Anti-apartheid revolutionary, political leader

Key Achievements

  1. Led the fight against apartheid in South Africa
  2. Spent 27 years in prison for his activism
  3. Became South Africa’s first Black president (1994–1999)
  4. Promoted reconciliation and peace
  5. Received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1993
